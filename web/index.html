Draft Duel · 2026/27

Two managers, one player pool. Take turns drafting a starting XI from the Süper Lig — whoever you pick is gone for your rival. Then GPT-6.1 Sol plays the match out minute by minute.

  1. Draft — host picks first, then you alternate until both have 11. Any position, any order.
  2. Ready — the joining player presses Ready, the host presses Simulate.
  3. Watch — GPT-6.1 Sol (high reasoning, on the host's ChatGPT plan) simulates the match on a neutral ground. Pause, rewind, and download it as a video.
